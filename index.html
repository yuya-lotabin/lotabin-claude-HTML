05 — Common questions

The details, upfront.

When does turnaround start?+

Turnaround begins once the brief is submitted, required assets are received, and scope is approved.

What counts as a light adjustment vs a new pack?+

Light adjustment credits cover minor updates to approved videos — hook swap, CTA change, caption update, pacing trim, music swap, small visual replacement, extra format version. A new product, new offer, new concept, new script, or full creative rework counts as a new Video Ad Pack.

What delivery formats do I get?+

Standard delivery is 9:16 with 1:1 included. Other exports (16:9, square variations) can be quoted or included depending on plan and scope.

Do you handle media buying or platform management?+

No. This is a video-only production desk. You (or your media buyer) handle strategy, ad accounts, and reporting. We supply the creative.

How does white-label work for agencies?+

We produce under your brand, in your delivery format, with a partner-facing handoff. Your client sees your agency. We stay behind the scenes. No client poaching.

How do you communicate?+

Clear intake, structured async updates via Loom, scheduled review checkpoints, fewer unnecessary meetings.
